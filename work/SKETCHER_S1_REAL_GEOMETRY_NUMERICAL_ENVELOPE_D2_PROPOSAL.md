## 0. Owner discussion: practical model envelope, not Snap/Copy/constraints

Owner's practical target, as an **indicative support envelope**, is:
- model of characteristic span **1 m = 1,000 mm**, minimum significant detail approximately **0.1 mm**;
- model of characteristic span **100 m = 100,000 mm**, minimum significant detail approximately **1 mm**.

These are expected reliable model scales, **not hard minimum entity lengths, not display-pixel thresholds, and not geometry that may be automatically merged**. Drawing/Copy/Move/Rotate remain free and unchanged; solver/Tangent constraints/OSNAP are separate future work and OUT OF SCOPE.

At binary64 absolute coordinate magnitude ~1,000 mm, one representable increment is ~1.1368683772161603e-13 mm; at ~100,000 mm it is ~1.4551915228366852e-11 mm. Illustrative **32 ULP** budgets are ~3.64e-12 and ~4.66e-10 mm respectively, vastly below 0.1/1 mm. **32 ULP is an illustrative calibration candidate, NOT a proven algorithmic bound or an approved constant.** A naïve fixed 1e-12 mm cannot uniformly cover both scales, and applying a fixed 1e-12 to a discriminant in units of mm^4 is dimensionally incorrect. An absolute 1e-12 floor also conflates representational error and intentionally authored real subpicometer gaps; this is a semantic choice, not a pure arithmetic improvement.

## 2. Proposed two-stage numerical semantics

**N0 — robust exact-on-stored-input predicate (uncontroversial B02 repair).** Give priority to a scale-conditioned error bound for sign of the Line↔Circle/Arc discriminant (and endpoint predicates). If the floating sign is provably outside the *computed arithmetic error bound*, retain strict sign; when inconclusive, recompute with higher/extended precision or an exact expansion/rational sign based on the original binary64 operands. Avoid arbitrary fixed \`epsilon\` on the discriminant. Preserve finite source range checks and exact tangent-vs-two-proper-crossings meaning. The fallback should distinguish **calculation rounding** from **authored nonintersecting binary values**. Compare case 1 vs 2 and all reversed/translated/rotated/scale variants.

**N1 — *separate* proposed represented-near-contact policy, only if explicitly Owner accepted.** For user-level geometry with independently authored small differences after Copy, exact arithmetic alone CANNOT make two distinct persisted coordinates identical. Investigate a scale-aware **numerical ambiguity band** (initial candidate \`max(1e-12 mm, k*ulp(M))\`, test k near 32) in linear-distance units, additionally bounded by a tiny fraction (initial study limit 1e-6) of the Owner's minimum significant feature envelope. \`M\` must describe relevant *absolute source coordinates*, with carefully translated/local algebra to avoid cancellation; the bound must be measured against errors of the actual evaluated predicate, NOT blindly applied to all edges/gaps. If a near relation cannot be resolved into one unique current topological contact with both incident source parameters in finite range, remain fail-closed. No implicit author-coordinate edits, no saved dummy EntityIds, no UI shift and no global tolerance escalation. **IMPORTANT:** regarding a *truly disjoint* persisted line as a topological contact is an observable semantics change. It conflicts with the original exact "a small gap remains a gap" rule and therefore MUST have exact standalone Owner D2 approval, plus consistent downstream Profile and native OCCT evidence, not just a green Trim screenshot.

**N2 — consistency and non-welding proofs.** One common relation classification must serve Shared-2D Region, Circle/Arc Trim, persistent Profile semantic anchor replay and native OCCT geometry. Do not independently add "Trim epsilon" or "Profile epsilon." Preserve shared boundary positive-length connectivity and external tangent Circle/Circle distinct material islands. If N1 tolerance only permits picking/diagnostics but native exact geometry has no consistent closed topology, **do not claim the edit/Extrude valid**; either devise a deterministic runtime derived-contact canonicalization that all layers can replay without modifying source, or report unsupported/ambiguous. Any need for a Kernel/serializer/public API/schema or other source path requires another STOP/Owner D2, not stealth widening.

**Single-contact-seam proposal.** Compute strict actual intersections and N0a exact binary contacts first. For unmatched pairs, consider a derived numerical contact only inside a *dimensionally correct, local linear-distance* error band. Initial study candidate (not an accepted fixed tolerance): T = max(1e-12 mm, 32 * ulp(M)), where M is a representative magnitude of the actual absolute binary64 coordinates involved, capped to no more than 1e-6 of the support envelope's minimum significant feature (0.1 mm at model span 1 m; 1 mm at span 100 m). Calibrate the numerical constant from tests, preserve separate rigorous arithmetic-sign bounds, and never compare the dimensional distance T directly with a quadratic discriminant. At 100 m, illustrative 32 ULP is only approximately 4.66e-10 mm; a genuine 1e-8 mm gap is much larger and **must remain open**. The near-contact choice is an acknowledged extension of the old strict no-gap semantics requiring its own exact Owner D2; it cannot be disguised as exact arithmetic.

**One unambiguous canonical *runtime* vertex.** For each eligible endpoint/curve near-contact, require one unique source pair, canonical contact branch and bounded support parameter; reject overlaps, multiple plausible candidates and genuine separated double roots that would otherwise collapse. A candidate nearest point may be projected onto an analytic Circle for the derived evaluated boundary ONLY: keep all authored Line/Circle/Arc coordinates, EntityIds, source roles and durable ProfileIntent references intact. If the current Kernel input or wire builder cannot represent it consistently without new public schema, STOP and request a new narrowly scoped D2 rather than writing an independent OCCT tolerance.

**Material semantics never follow point-only equivalence.** Two externally tangent whole Circles still yield distinct material islands and disconnected Add Area; only a positive-length actual shared edge can join material. Real gaps larger than the calibrated band remain gaps. If an unexpected ambiguity appears, reject the operation and preserve Document state rather than fabricating a Face.
