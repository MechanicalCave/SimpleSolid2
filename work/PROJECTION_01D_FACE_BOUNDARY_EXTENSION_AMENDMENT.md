Each click expands **only that exact selected Face** into native outer and inner Face-wire Edge occurrences. **No automatic adjacent Face selection, carrier-based traversal, Surface unification, planar similarity merge, or construction of an outer perimeter of the union**. Selection is transient, editable/removable before Finish, and must have current provider/Body generation and Part stage identity.
2. Within each chosen Face, retain **every individually certified authorable material Edge**, including a genuine material Edge shared with another selected Face. A curve that would require an unsupported conic/spline, invalid exact geometric image or unsupported exact projection is **geometric Unsupported** and is **skipped individually**, with explicit source Edge/Face, typed reason, visible red highlighting and accepted/skipped counts. Supported neighbors and supported members of other selected Faces remain staged. **An open contour, multiple disconnected segments or a non-Profile result is NOT a failure condition**. Never fabricate closing Edges, approximate unsupported geometry, tolerant-snap, heal, or silently change the authored Sketch/Profile semantics.
4. **At least one** accepted unique supported Edge permits **one** existing `CreateProjectedSketchEdgesCommand` atomic Finish, even if other members are geometrically Unsupported. Regular/Construction is still selected as before; one transaction, one Undo/Redo and cold linked v15 behavior remain mandatory. If there are **zero** eligible Edges, disable/refuse Finish with an explanatory no-op (no empty commit).
5. This is **partial by geometry, never by guessed identity**. For any clicked Face, ambiguous/missing bounded Face identity, mismatched provider/generation/stage, broken or incomplete native wire accounting, uncertified material/nonmaterial classification, stale transaction/revision or missing provider produces a typed **integrity failure**, not a skippable geometric curve. Reject a bad *new selection* while preserving earlier valid staged choices; at Finish revalidate the same exact staged per-Face/per-Edge membership and skip decisions. If the previously accepted selection changes or becomes unverifiable, reject the **entire Finish** without mutating the document. Uniquely certified known non-material seams/partitions are excluded, not falsely reported as unsupported material curves.
6. Highlight each selected bounded Face and accepted/skipped current source Edges; display distinct selected-Face count, unique material Edge count, accepted count, skipped geometry count, excluded nonmaterial count and typed blocked reason. Support selecting multiple Faces, removing a chosen Face, manual Edge coexistence, deduplication and Cancel/Esc/focus parity with the existing single tool state. ## 1. Frozen product intent

One `PROJECT` tool with three **source-acquisition modes**, all delegating the resulting unique, exact material Edges to the existing `CreateProjectedSketchEdgesCommand`:

1. **Edges:** pick individual current material Edges; existing PG-01C.
2. **Planar Face:** keep the current strict, *one bounded planar provider Face* outer/hole wire mechanism, diagnostics, tests and fail-closed gate; existing PG-01D.
3. **Face Boundary (new):** a clicked *surface area* is a temporary **selection definition**, not a new projected surface nor an authored Face dependency. Collect the material Edge occurrences forming the perimeter (outer and actual openings) of its logically contiguous **material surface region**, regardless of whether that surface is planar, oblique, cylindrical or otherwise curved. Only those Edges, not the surface itself, are independently projected on the active Sketch support.

If the provider partitions **one certifiably continuous semantic surface region** into several bounded Faces, traverse exactly those genuine same-region fragments and **exclude strictly certified internal representation/partition edges** from the selected perimeter. Do **not** join separate real material regions solely due to coplanarity, tangency, proximity, matching color or shared coordinates. A real boundary, opening, hard edge, change in semantic surface intent, or failed region-continuity proof stops the traversal. Multiple material Edges that happen to project to equal coordinates remain distinct semantic sources; manual Edge+Face Boundary duplicates are removed only by exact `MaterialEdgeReference` equality.
